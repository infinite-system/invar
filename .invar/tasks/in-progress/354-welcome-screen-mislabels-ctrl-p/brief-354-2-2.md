# Brief 354-2 — your welcome-text change broke the move-line smoke

## In plain words

The move-line drive times out on your branch and passes at the merge
base. Your welcome message grew from 4 rows to 5 (the new "F1 Show All
Commands" line). A smoke that counts rows through the empty-editor
welcome state is now off by one. Find the real mechanism by driving,
then fix the smoke or the layout — whichever is actually wrong.

## Evidence (conductor)

- Merge base: `bun scripts/harness/smoke-move-line-harness.ts` PASS.
  Your branch: FAIL at awaitGridCondition (PtyTestDriver.ts:483),
  retried and failed in the gate, and failed again on a solo re-gate.
- Your diff added one welcome row (SourceTextPaneContent: the block is
  now 5 lines, was 4). The move-line harness paints `[Files]` in its
  status at failure — it may be sitting on the welcome/empty state when
  it counts.

## Ranked candidates (drive to separate — do NOT assume one)

1. The move-line smoke navigates by an ABSOLUTE row that assumed the
   4-row welcome block; the 5th row shifts its target. Fix: the smoke
   derives the row from screen content, not a hardcoded number.
2. The smoke reads the welcome text to locate a start position and your
   reworded line broke its needle.
3. A shared layout constant (welcome block height) feeds content
   positioning and genuinely moved the editor origin — a real app
   regression, not a smoke fix. Drive to rule this in or out.

## Method (paired, not single-sample)

Run move-line at the merge base AND on your branch, 3 times each,
alternating — confirm the base-green / branch-red split is real and not
one flaky sample (the conductor ran base only once). Then drive the
failing step and read which row the smoke expects vs where the content
paints.

## The bar

Fix the ACTUAL wrong thing (smoke arithmetic if the layout is fine; the
layout if content truly shifted). No timeout widening. The welcome text
itself is correct and stays — do not revert the label fix.

## Invariants in scope

Editor/welcome layout records if any; the move-line smoke's row
derivation is the likely fix site.
